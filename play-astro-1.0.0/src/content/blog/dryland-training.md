Add leg lifts, shoulder taps, or stability ball challenges to mimic the instability of the water.",

These exercises build strength across multiple muscle groups and mirror the pulling, pushing, and kicking motions of swimming. Resistance bands are also invaluable—they allow you to replicate swim-specific movements with added load, strengthening your catch, pull, and recovery phases without the impact of heavy weights.",

These movements build the fast-twitch muscle fibers that fire during a dive or a breakout. Start with bodyweight exercises, then progress to added resistance.
